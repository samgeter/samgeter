I’m **Samuel Getachew**, a software engineer and founder based in Addis Ababa. I build AI agents, knowledge systems, and software products from the first prototype through production. At **Yuba Labs**, I lead development, workflow evaluation, and platform performance across products for founders, entrepreneurship programs, and investment teams.

## Selected work

- **[Yuba](https://www.samuel-getachew.et/work/yuba/)** — I co-designed and led a venture-building platform that guides founders from problem discovery through market validation. I also built workflow evaluations and Locust load tests for the platform.
- **[YubaVC](https://www.samuel-getachew.et/work/yubavc/) · Prototype** — I prototyped a research workflow that combines web research and fact-checking into structured investment-readiness reports for human review.
- **[Apptus](https://www.samuel-getachew.et/work/apptus/)** — I designed and built a founder recruitment platform where programs can tailor applications, assess submissions against a shared rubric, and shortlist candidates.
- **[Metus](https://www.samuel-getachew.et/work/metus/)** — I built a Three.js experience with five scroll-controlled scenes showing how whey from cheese production could be recovered for protein and animal feed.
- **[Dossie](https://www.samuel-getachew.et/work/dossie/)** — I designed an enterprise knowledge platform to make information across physical records, documents, and databases searchable in one place.
- **[DossieClean](https://www.samuel-getachew.et/work/dossieclean/) · Internal tool** — I built a PDF processing workflow for metadata review, chunking, and semantic search. It handled most of the document preparation behind DossieScholar’s collection of 15,000 university research papers.
